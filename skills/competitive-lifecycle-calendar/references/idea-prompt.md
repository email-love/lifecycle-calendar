# Evidence-grounded campaign ideas (on demand, per moment)

Ideas are not pre-written into the calendar. Generate them one moment at a time, when the user is planning that moment, so each idea takes real inputs: the brand's actual mechanics and the moment's competitive evidence.

## Inputs to gather before generating

- The moment: name, date, relevance, overview, and its Competitive evidence section.
- Brand facts, gathered once per session and reused: app, loyalty program, SMS, subscription, retail presence, brand voice.

## The framework

Three angles per moment, one for each job. If two ideas share an emotion, an audience, or a metric, they are the same idea written twice; cut and redo. (The three-job split follows the Engage / Convert / Retain framing from Luke Kline's lifecycle-calendar skill, credited here.)

1. **Volume**: the broad-list play everyone expects, run because it works.
2. **Conversion**: the urgency play for the ready and the on-the-fence.
3. **Loyalty**: the play that makes best and lapsed customers feel chosen.

## Each idea is five lines

- **Angle**: the one-line concept.
- **Move**: what actually sends, using only mechanics this brand has. No inventing capabilities.
- **Send window**: taken from the evidence, not guessed. If competitors start four weeks out, the window says so. Sends land before or on the moment, never after.
- **Offer guardrail**: what the evidence supports. If the observed range for this moment is limited products with no discounting, say so and do not propose 30 percent off. If the deep discount is reserved for BFCM, protect that.
- **Metric**: what it should move, different for each of the three ideas.

## Open-lane moments

If the moment's evidence section says no competitor is claiming it, say so in the ideas: the upside is an uncontested inbox, the risk is that the moment may not pull, so recommend a light test before committing budget.

## Stretch moments

Stretch moments still get three real ideas plus one honest line: this is a reach for the brand, run it only with a genuinely clever take, otherwise spend the attention on a Core moment.

## Writing into Notion

Append under the moment's Competitive evidence section as `## Campaign ideas`, three short sections, five lines each. Do not overwrite the evidence.
